4 · Why the accent can’t be brass

R4

Brass is the obvious ship accent, and it is the one thing here I’d push back on. R4 chooses blue specifically so the accent can never be confused with a status hue. Brass #C9A227 lands 7° from --status-warn #F2B33D. “This row is selected” and “the harness intervened” would become the same colour.

Same colour, two meanings.

✓ brass as chrome — legal

R32b reserves status hues for status and puts chrome on the --line ramp. So brass is fine in seams and fittings — it just can’t carry meaning. That is where the ship reads from anyway.
